# Modules

Registry of module IDs used by `.agents/maps/`, `.agents/decisions/` and `MODULE:` Decision blocks.

## Structural modules

- `engine` — CPU engine: `core/engine/` except the page table and TLB fill/flush (Tier 0 interpreter `interp.c`/`simd.c`/`atomic.c`/`native.c`/`cpu.c`, Tier 1 translator `dbt.c`/`emit.h`, `features.c`, `guard.c`, `include/tuxport/engine.h`, Rust bindings `src/lib.rs`) plus `tests/lockstep`; instruction execution, translation, code cache and sharing, FP state, CPU features, host fault guard.
- `kernel/process` — processes, threads and scheduling: `core/kernel/src/task.rs`, `process.rs`, `kernel.rs` (BKL, `Cx`, wake-ups; except the `INIT` constant in kernel/boot and `Shared::stop_checks`/`resume_checks` in kernel/time), `cell.rs`, `syscall/mod.rs` (the dispatcher, except its restart and unimplemented-syscall handling and kernel/time's time helpers), `syscall/proc.rs` (except its execve and unshare sections), `syscall/fast.rs` (except its futex call), `syscall/misc.rs` (except what kernel/signal, kernel/futex, kernel/ns, kernel/time and kernel/boot list there: resource use and limits and the remaining process-wide calls), `machine.rs` except kernel/boot's lifecycle types/functions and kernel/time's `timers`, `watchdog.rs`, `bug.rs`, `syscall/sched.rs`; task lifecycle, syscall dispatch, BKL, its watchdog and the fast path, kernel bugs and kill reports, waiting, host process statistics and wait helpers.
- `kernel/time` — clocks, sleeps and timers: `core/kernel/src/itimer.rs`, `syscall/posix_timer.rs`, `loadavg.rs`, `machine.rs`'s `timers`, `Shared::stop_checks`/`resume_checks` in `kernel.rs`, `read_timespec`, `timespec` and `KTIME_MAX` in `syscall/mod.rs`, and in `syscall/misc.rs` `nanosleep`, `clock_nanosleep`, `sleep_rest`, `sleep_until`, `getitimer`, `setitimer`, `check_itimer`, `read_itimerval`, `itimer_value`, `write_itimerval`, `clock_gettime`, `clock_getres`, `cpu_clock` and `gettimeofday`; deadline bases and saturation, clock jumps, interval and POSIX timers, CPU-time-limit expiry, load averages and the timer thread's periodic work. CPU accounting, resource-limit syscalls, generic waits and the fast path remain kernel/process's; time-namespace offsets remain kernel/ns's; clock setting, timezone and restart dispatch remain kernel/signal's; timerfd remains kernel/poll's.
- `kernel/boot` — machine lifecycle and virtual init: `core/kernel/src/inittab.rs`, the `INIT` constant in `kernel.rs`, `reboot` in `syscall/misc.rs`, and in `machine.rs` `MachineOptions`, `Stdio`, the `Machine` owner, `SHUTDOWN_TIMEOUT`, `Machine::start`/`start_kernel`, `boot`, `spawn`/`spawn_process`, `shutdown`, `inittab`, `run_init_process`, `kill_all`, `take_power`, `Drop for Machine` and `run`; kernel startup, host-launched programs, PID 1's boot and power policy, inittab and shutdown. Shared kernel fields, general reparenting/reaping, host wait helpers, process statistics and the remaining Machine methods stay with kernel/process; timer work stays with kernel/time, signal syscall call sites with kernel/signal and console behaviour with kernel/tty.
- `kernel/exec` — program execution: `core/kernel/src/loader.rs` and the execve section of `syscall/proc.rs`; execve and execveat (the argument limit, targets, `#!` scripts, `ETXTBSY`, the effects of set-id bits and file capabilities), the ELF loader (checks, doomed images, segment mapping, placement, the initial stack and auxv), the point of no return, the process's `exe_file`, the executable cache and the names of the program's mappings.
- `kernel/ns` — namespaces: `core/kernel/src/nsproxy.rs`, `pidns.rs`, `syscall/ns.rs`, the unshare section of `syscall/proc.rs`, the time-namespace offsets in `syscall/misc.rs` and user namespaces in `cred.rs`; creating namespaces at clone and unshare, setns and the namespace-file ioctls, user namespaces' ID maps and the translation of IDs shown outward, pid namespaces' tables, time-namespace offsets and their vDSO stubs, UTS and cgroup namespaces, namespace counts and limits, and nsfs files.
- `kernel/futex` — futexes: `core/kernel/src/futex.rs`, the futex and robust-list handlers in `syscall/misc.rs` and the futex call of `syscall/fast.rs`; keys, the waiter queue the BKL and fast paths share, the futex commands with `FUTEX_WAKE_OP` and `futex_waitv`, PI locks and their hand-over when an owner exits, robust lists and `clear_child_tid` wakes.
- `kernel/signal` — signals and the user-visible ABI: `core/kernel/src/signal.rs`, `syscall/sig.rs`, `coredump.rs`, `klog.rs`, `stub.rs`, `errno.rs`, `debug.rs`, signalfd in `fd.rs`/`syscall/sig.rs`, the restart and unimplemented-syscall handling in `syscall/mod.rs`, the fault-to-signal branch in `task.rs`, and uname/syslog/adjtimex/clock setting/restart_syscall in `syscall/misc.rs`; delivery, frames and sigreturn, restart, faults, core dumps, kernel logging, the Linux 6.1 ABI and ENOSYS policy, stub counts and host debug tracing. Ptrace implementation is kernel/ptrace and vDSO image construction is kernel/vdso; signal-side calls into both remain here.
- `kernel/ptrace` — guest tracing: `core/kernel/src/ptrace.rs` and `syscall/ptrace.rs`; tracer requests and tracee stops, saved registers, hardware debug slots, traced exits and process_vm_readv/writev. Signal/task/dispatcher/clone/exec/wait/seccomp call sites remain in their existing modules; procfs memory-file handling stays kernel/procfs's, access policy kernel/cred's and memory writes kernel/mm's.
- `kernel/vdso` — guest vDSO images: `core/kernel/src/vdso.rs`; host-image discovery/validation, fallback ELF, signal-return trampoline and clock-stub detection. Image mapping and auxv stay kernel/exec/kernel/mm's, time-namespace switching kernel/ns's, clock syscalls kernel/time's and fast dispatch kernel/process's.
- `kernel/store` — the rootfs inode store: `core/kernel/src/vfs/store.rs`, `vfs/import.rs`, `vfs/browse.rs` and the host wrappers in `lib.rs` (`import_rootfs`, `sync_store`, `browse`, `browse_changing`, `write_file`, `read_file`); SQLite namespace and objects, deferred commits and power-loss ordering, the reaper and checkpoints, boot cleanup, the metadata cache, sharing with the DocumentsProvider, rootfs import, the host browse API.
- `kernel/vfs` — the VFS layer: `core/kernel/src/vfs/mod.rs` (except the mount table and the overlay methods), `vfs/dev.rs`, `syscall/fs.rs` (except kernel/files' parts, its overlay functions, `flock`, the fcntl lock commands and the pipe, FIFO and splice code: `getdents64`, the sections Metadata, stat, Changing the namespace, chmod, chown and utimensat, Paths and tmpfs accounting, and the remaining shared helpers), and in `syscall/mount.rs` the tmpfs options; mount-aware nodes and path resolution (openat2 `RESOLVE_*`, the no-BKL `Cached` resolver), mount-flag checks in file operations, tmpfs instances, metadata and namespace syscalls, `/dev` population and device numbers, and the calls into the overlay and inotify code.
- `kernel/files` — open files: `core/kernel/src/fd.rs` (its `EventFd` and `TimerFd` are kernel/poll's), and in `syscall/fs.rs` the section Opening, closing, duplicating (except `open_overlay`, `flock`, the fcntl lock commands, `pipe2`, `open_fifo` and the pipe-size fcntl commands), the section Reading and writing (except `follow_copy_up`, `copy_up`, the pipe reads and writes and the splice block), `lseek`, the sections Cache hints and syncing and truncate and fallocate, and the shared helpers `executing`, `open_for_write`, `check_seals`, `remove_privs` and `truncating`; fd tables and open file descriptions, fcntl and the generic ioctls, opening paths (magic-link reopens, file handles, memfd, character devices by number), data I/O with the `f_pos_lock` emulation, sendfile and copy_file_range, clones and object unsharing, truncate/ftruncate/fallocate, cache hints and syncing.
- `kernel/overlay` — overlayfs: `core/kernel/src/vfs/overlay.rs`, the overlay methods of `Vfs` in `vfs/mod.rs`, `open_overlay`, `follow_copy_up`, `copy_up`, `unlink_overlay`, `link_overlay` and `rename_overlay` in `syscall/fs.rs`, and in `syscall/mount.rs` the overlay options and the overlay node sweep; mounting and layers, lookup through layers with whiteouts and opaque directories, node lifetime and the sweep, merged readdir and stat, copy-up, creating, removing and renaming through an overlay, and files opened through one.
- `kernel/locks` — advisory locks and leases: `core/kernel/src/locks.rs`, `syscall/lease.rs`, and `flock` and the fcntl lock commands in `syscall/fs.rs`; flock, POSIX and OFD locks with ranges, waits and deadlock detection, leases and their breaks, and the `/proc/locks` and fdinfo listings.
- `kernel/pipe` — pipes and splicing: `core/kernel/src/pipe.rs`, and in `syscall/fs.rs` `pipe2`, `open_fifo`, the pipe-size fcntl commands, pipe reads and writes and the splice block (`splice` to `wait_splice_done`); pipe buffers and capacity, FIFO opens, `SIGPIPE`, and splice, tee and vmsplice with their checks and waits.
- `kernel/xattr` — extended attributes: `core/kernel/src/syscall/xattr.rs`; the xattr syscalls, name prefixes and permissions, POSIX ACLs stored but not enforced, and `security.capability`'s checks and conversions.
- `kernel/mount` — mounts and mount namespaces: `core/kernel/src/syscall/mount.rs` (except the tmpfs and overlay options and the overlay node sweep) and the mount table in `vfs/mod.rs` (`Mount`, `Mounts`, `MntNs`, `Propagation`, `Index`, `GroupAlloc` and the `Vfs` methods that add, copy, move, propagate, unmount and collect mounts and namespaces); boot mounts, mount(2) with binds, remounts and moves, umount2, pivot_root, open_tree/move_mount/mount_setattr, superblock flags, propagation between peers and slaves, mount locks, `fs.mount-max`, copying, entering and collecting mount namespaces, and the mount data mountinfo shows.
- `kernel/inotify` — inotify: `core/kernel/src/syscall/inotify.rs`; instances, watches and their per-user limits, the per-kernel registry keyed by store inode or by overlay, FUSE or cgroup2 node key, the hooks at Linux's fsnotify call sites and the events they report, the `Opened` record of open files, and replaying the Files app's and cgroup2 files' changes as events.
- `kernel/aio` — native AIO: `core/kernel/src/syscall/aio.rs`; io_setup to io_pgetevents, contexts per thread group and their `aio-nr` charge, synchronous reads, writes and fsyncs at submission, pending `IOCB_CMD_POLL`s completed by io_getevents, io_cancel or the timer thread, and context release at execve and exit.
- `kernel/loop` — loop devices: `core/kernel/src/syscall/loopdev.rs`; `/dev/loop-control` and `/dev/loop<N>` (the only block devices), openers and exclusive claims, binding to a backing file, status, capacity and block-size requests, I/O forwarded to the backing file at the device offset, block ioctls, and the `/sys/block/loop<N>` values.
- `kernel/fuse` — FUSE: `core/kernel/src/vfs/fuse.rs`, `syscall/fuse.rs`; filesystems a guest daemon serves through `/dev/fuse` (Linux 6.1's `fs/fuse`): mount options and connections, INIT negotiation, requests and replies with interrupts and aborts, the device file (read, write, splice, poll, clone), the node cache path resolution reads and the syscall retry that fills it, and the file syscalls on FUSE nodes (open and create, I/O, directories, namespace and attribute changes, xattrs, flushes and releases, copies for mappings and execve).
- `kernel/procfs` — synthetic filesystem views: `core/kernel/src/pseudo.rs`; procfs (per-process and system-wide files, `/proc/sys` sysctls and their namespace scoping, `/proc/<pid>/net`), sysfs, devpts and nsfs nodes, the mqueue filesystem's nodes, and the cgroup2 node adapters for lookup, metadata, listing and dispatch to kernel/cgroup. Cgroup policy and device BPF belong to kernel/cgroup.
- `kernel/cgroup` — cgroup v2 and device BPF: `core/kernel/src/cgroup.rs`, `bpf.rs`, `syscall/bpf.rs`; the cgroup tree and interface files, membership and migration, controller delegation and enforced limits, freezing and killing, change observation for poll and inotify, and the bpf(2) subset for cgroup device programs (verification, attachment and execution). Synthetic node adapters remain kernel/procfs's; namespace creation, mount creation, file-syscall call sites, the freezer wait and OOM victim selection remain with kernel/ns, kernel/mount, kernel/files and kernel/vfs, kernel/signal and kernel/oom respectively.
- `kernel/tty` — terminals: `core/kernel/src/tty.rs`, `syscall/tty.rs`, `terminal.rs`; in-process PTY pairs (allocation, opening, hangup and vhangup), the line discipline and termios/termios2, packet mode, job control (controlling terminals, foreground groups, background checks, terminal signals), and the host console used by the app's tabs and the `tuxport-run` bridge.
- `kernel/net` — the socket layer for internet sockets and the socket syscalls every family goes through: `core/kernel/src/net.rs` (except its netlink sections), `syscall/net.rs` (except its `netlink_*` functions, its netlink section and the `Socket::Netlink` arms), `rawicmp.rs`, `route.rs`, `portmap.rs`, `traffic.rs`, `origdst.rs`; TCP/UDP passthrough to host sockets with emulated blocking and options, raw ICMP on ping sockets, the routing table, the DNS stub, low-port mapping and the inbound forwarding hooks, traffic counters and the `/proc/net` socket, protocol and route files, `SO_ORIGINAL_DST` and `SO_MARK`.
- `kernel/netlink` — netlink sockets in process: in `core/kernel/src/net.rs` the sections netlink, rtnetlink notifications, `NETLINK_GENERIC`, Netlink messages and `NETLINK_SOCK_DIAG`; in `syscall/net.rs` the `netlink_*` functions, the netlink section (`netlink_send`, `rtnl_watched`, `CHANGING_IOCTLS`, `rtnl_notify`, `KernelHost`) and the `Socket::Netlink` arms of the socket calls; binding, groups and options, request handling and reply queues, rtnetlink queries and change requests, link, address and route notifications, `nlctrl`, `NETLINK_SOCK_DIAG`, the `NETLINK_NETFILTER` entry point and `NETLINK_KOBJECT_UEVENT`.
- `kernel/netns` — network namespaces without a network stack: `core/kernel/src/netns.rs`, `netdev.rs`, `forward.rs`, `netconf.rs`; host loopback blocks and address translation, namespace ports/listeners, virtual devices with routes/sysctls, packet construction and hook routing into kernel/nft, and forwarding outside connections to published ports. The ruleset control plane and expression evaluator in nft.rs belong to kernel/nft; socket and netlink call sites remain kernel/net/kernel/netlink's.
- `kernel/nft` — nftables rulesets and evaluation: `core/kernel/src/nft.rs`; NETLINK_NETFILTER batches, tables/chains/rules/sets, query encoding and synthetic-packet expression execution. Namespace path construction, hook selection and forwarding stay kernel/netns's; socket verdict application and netlink transport stay kernel/net/kernel/netlink's.
- `kernel/unix` — AF_UNIX sockets emulated in process: `core/kernel/src/unix.rs`, `syscall/unix.rs`; stream, datagram and seqpacket sockets, path and abstract names, connection lifecycle, data transfer with `SCM_RIGHTS`/`SCM_CREDENTIALS`/`SO_PEERCRED`, readiness, `/proc/net/unix`, and the garbage collector for in-flight cycles.
- `kernel/poll` — I/O multiplexing: `core/kernel/src/syscall/poll.rs` and `syscall/timerfd.rs`; the shared readiness model (`readiness`, `arrivals`, `wake_objects`), ppoll and pselect6, epoll (edge triggering, nesting, ONESHOT, EXCLUSIVE gates), eventfd2, timerfd, and signal-driven I/O checked by the timer thread.
- `kernel/ipc` — System V IPC and POSIX message queues: `core/kernel/src/ipc.rs`, `syscall/ipc.rs`, `syscall/mqueue.rs`; per-IPC-namespace object tables and limits, SysV ids and `ipc_perm` checks, message queues, semaphores with `SEM_UNDO`, shared memory segments and their destruction, the `mq_*` syscalls and the message-queue objects (priority order, blocking, notification) behind the mqueue filesystem, and the contents of `/proc/sysvipc`.
- `kernel/cred` — credentials, capabilities and seccomp: `core/kernel/src/cred.rs` (except user namespaces), `syscall/cred.rs`, `seccomp.rs`; the credential structure and id syscalls, capability scopes, `capset` and the capability prctls, securebits, the execve credential transition with file capabilities, DAC and ownership helpers, signal and ptrace access checks, and guest seccomp (strict mode, cBPF filters, actions, TSYNC, user notifications, the always-allow cache).
- `kernel/mm` — guest memory: `core/kernel/src/mm.rs` (except what kernel/mmu and kernel/oom list there), `core/kernel/src/syscall/mem.rs` (except process_mrelease), `core/kernel/src/uaccess.rs` (`UserSlice`), and the page-fault branch of the task run loop; address spaces and VMAs, host backing and its release, the memory syscalls, brk and stack growth, the fault policy, fork copy-on-write, file mappings at EOF and truncation, FUSE mappings read on demand, and the maps and core-dump views.
- `kernel/mmu` — the soft-MMU: the page table in `core/engine/src/c/mmu.c` and the `Mmu` wrapper, the software TLB in `core/engine/src/c/cpu.c` and `internal.h`, and in `core/kernel/src/mm.rs` the `Consistency and code` section and the `Mm` methods that fill entries (`map_pages`, `fault_in`) and keep threads consistent (`add_user`, `remove_user`, `generations`, `code_space`, `invalidate_code`, `sync`, `quiesce`, `shootdown`); page-table entries and their population, the TLB and its Tier 0 refill, shootdowns and quiescing, the code-change log, and code groups sharing translations.
- `kernel/oom` — memory accounting and the OOM killer: `core/kernel/src/oom.rs`, the resident-memory measures (`resident_in`, `cow_shared`, `pagemap`, `stack_bytes`, `resident_pages`, `peak_resident_pages`, `resident_split`, `Usage`) and `Mm::release` in `mm.rs`, and process_mrelease in `syscall/mem.rs`; resident memory for procfs, rusage and child cgroups, the app-wide and cgroup limits, victim choice and kills, `oom_score`, process_mrelease, and the release of written code views.
- `hal` — the host abstraction layer: `core/hal/` (`lib.rs`, `net.rs`); one implementation for Android (bionic) and Linux (glibc) hosts returning raw host errnos: host memory mappings and residency, file and storage calls on the object directory, host statistics and CPUs, polling with `Event`/`Edges`/`Watch`, per-thread host controls and clocks, host sockets, interfaces, routes and DNS, and the CLI's terminal helpers.
- `android/service` — the app's foreground service: `android/app/src/main/java/io/github/sajadxdp0/tuxport/TerminalService.kt`, `BrowserBridge.kt`, `Notifications.kt`, `Settings.kt`, `Tabs.kt`, `Diagnostics.kt`; owning the kernel, browser endpoint and all sessions, start, boot, shutdown, reboot and distro replacement, the notification and wake lock, memory trimming, the 2 s poll of kernel events, kill and boot-failure reports, bounded diagnostic retention and archive sharing, and tab restore after the app was killed.
- `android/setup` — the guest system and logins: `android/app/src/main/java/io/github/sajadxdp0/tuxport/Rootfs.kt`, `SettingsActivity.kt`, `android/app/src/main/assets/login.sh`, `android/app/src/main/assets/profile.sh`, `android/app/src/main/assets/ashrc.sh`, `android/app/src/main/assets/bashrc.sh`, `android/app/src/main/assets/zshrc.sh`, `android/app/src/main/assets/xdg-open.sh`, `scripts/fetch-rootfs`; distributions and verified downloads, import and first-boot configuration (bash as the login shell of Alpine imports), per-start files, the guest browser launcher, the login shells' profile and its prompt marks, package mirrors, login account resolution, installing the bundled bash and first-login admin setup, the settings screen, and the store lock.
- `android/documents` — the Files app provider: `android/app/src/main/java/io/github/sajadxdp0/tuxport/Documents.kt`; home and whole-system roots, path document IDs, MIME detection, reads and home-limited writes through the store's browse API, grants, change notifications in both directions, and the documents the terminal's `file:` links name.
- `android/terminal` — terminal activity and shared UI: `MainActivity.kt`, `TerminalLayout.kt`, `TerminalLinks.kt`, `NotificationPermission.kt`, `BatteryOptimization.kt`, `SearchBarView.kt`, `Ui.kt` (in `android/app/src/main/java/io/github/sajadxdp0/tuxport/`) and `android/app/src/main/res/` except android/privacy's `layout/privacy_shield.xml`, `drawable/privacy_emblem.xml`, `drawable/ic_lock.xml` and android/licenses' `layout/list_item.xml`, `values/ids.xml`; lifecycle, session selection/closing, shortcuts, search UI, insets/IME animation, links, clipboard questions and all MainActivity control adapters/picker/dialog handling. Shared strings/themes/page.xml/preference_switch.xml and Ui.showPrivate remain here; tab rendering is android/tabbar, extra keys android/keys and retained file-copy work android/file-import.
- `android/tabbar` — tab controls and progress drawing: `TabBarView.kt`, `TabListView.kt`, `TabNumber.kt`, `TabMarks.kt`, `ProgressLine.kt` (in the same directory); bar/list rendering, session snapshots, number animations, status marks, accessible descriptions and the progress line. Activity callbacks, focus/Back policy and layout placement remain android/terminal's; shared ExtraKeysView indicator helpers are android/keys's.
- `android/keys` — terminal extra keys: `ExtraKeysView.kt` (in the same directory); adaptive rows/pages, touches and repeats, one-shot/locked modifiers, shared fade and indicator helpers consumed by android/tabbar. MainActivity input routing, privacy checks and TerminalLayout's insets/IME animation remain android/terminal's; encoding remains android/vtview's.
- `android/file-import` — retained terminal file imports: `TerminalFiles.kt` (in the same directory); picker request state, copy worker/results, sanitized collision-safe home-file creation and shell path escaping. MainActivity picker callbacks, result dialogs, final privacy/clipboard checks and shared resources remain android/terminal's; the button is android/tabbar's, store/account resolution android/setup's.
- `android/privacy` — app-wide authentication: `Privacy.kt`, `NotificationActivity.kt` (in the same directory), and `layout/privacy_shield.xml`, `drawable/privacy_emblem.xml` and `drawable/ic_lock.xml` under `android/app/src/main/res/`; process-local unlock state, activity lifecycle and screen-off locking, secure activity/dialog windows, the shield, authenticated mode changes, fresh diagnostic-export authorization and private notification report-copy authentication. Call-site enforcement, preferences, notification routing, export generation/handoff, application registration and tests remain with their existing modules.
- `android/licenses` — the notices UI: `LicensesActivity.kt`, `LicenseActivity.kt`, `LicenseCatalog.kt` (in the same directory), and `layout/list_item.xml` and `values/ids.xml` under `android/app/src/main/res/`; catalog parsing and localized summaries, the component list, offline HTML reading, local/external links and reading-position restoration. Shared UI resources remain android/terminal's; notice assets, their CSS, dependency inventory, build and manifest remain android/build's; instrumentation tests remain android/tests's.
- `android/vt` — the Kotlin terminal sessions: `VtSession.kt` and `ConsolePty.kt` (in the same directory); lazy console and login-shell startup, the pump/writer/waiter thread lifecycle, exactly-once console closing, session progress and status records, asynchronous clipboard answers and events to the service, transcript retention and restore fallback, and session-side messages, cursor settings and clear/reset calls. Native terminal behavior and JNI belong to android/vtcore.
- `android/vtcore` — the native terminal implementation: `VtNative.kt` (in the same directory), `hosts/android/src/vt.rs`, and `hosts/android/src/vt.c` except its frame-format specification and Frames section (android/vtrender) and Input, Paste, Mouse and focus, Selection, Links, Taps on the prompt and Search sections (android/vtview); terminal handles and locking, the console-output pump, protocol effects, shell-mark parsing and command timing, tmux detachment, PNG decoding, terminal options, messages, transcripts, snapshots and restoring, clearing and resetting, scrollback compression, image trimming, and all VtNative JNI functions. Kotlin session and console lifecycle remains android/vt's.
- `android/vtview` — the terminal view's lifecycle and interaction: `VtView.kt` and `VtKeys.kt` (in the same directory), and the Input, Paste, Mouse and focus, Selection, Links, Taps on the prompt and Search sections of `hosts/android/src/vt.c`; attaching sessions, requesting frames and applying their interaction state, redraw and cursor/password timers, keys, the IME and pastes, gestures, scrolling and flings, selection by touch and mouse, links, search, zoom, and jumping, selecting output and moving the cursor by prompt marks. All of `VtView`, including `onDraw`, `apply` and interaction overlays, remains here; rendering uses android/vtrender.
- `android/vtrender` — terminal frames and rendering: `VtFrame.kt`, `VtScreen.kt`, `VtRenderer.kt`, `VtImages.kt`, `VtGlyphs.kt` (in the same directory), the terminal font in `android/app/src/main/assets/fonts/`, and the frame-format specification and Frames section of `hosts/android/src/vt.c`; frame encoding/decoding, frame-driven search progress and viewport match encoding, Kitty placements and image extraction, the screen mirror and image cache, font metrics, and drawing text, images, the cursor and preedit. View lifecycle and frame orchestration remain android/vtview's; terminal ownership, PNG decoding, cursor defaults, JNI and locking remain android/vtcore's.
- `android/native` — runtime JNI and application entry: `hosts/android/src/lib.rs`, `hosts/android/open-url.c`, `Native.kt` and `MainApplication.kt` (in the same Kotlin directory); kernel handle table, Native JNI, error/panic mapping, stderr-to-logcat, app-wide component installation and guest browser client. Runner/build/debug manifest configuration stays android/build's; runtime behavior stays with its implementation module.
- `harness` — development orchestration and standalone test tools: `tests/bench/`, `tests/survey/`, `tests/crash/`, `scripts/dev`, `scripts/dev.Dockerfile`, `scripts/build-ltp`, `scripts/ltp`, `scripts/survey`, `.github/workflows/`; benchmarks, LTP, workload surveys, power-loss tests, development container and CI. CLI/integration tests are harness/cli's and differential/upstream suites harness/diff's.

Kernel run/boot/console semantics remain kernel/boot/kernel/tty's; benchmark workloads remain harness's.
- `harness/diff` — differential and upstream syscall suites: `scripts/difftest`, `tests/diff/`, `scripts/build-starnix-tests`, `tests/starnix/`, `scripts/build-gvisor-tests`, `tests/gvisor/`; native comparison, stores/lanes/locking, case inputs and skips, suite builders and gtest runner. Prebuilt tuxport-run is harness/cli's; development container, LTP and CI are harness's.
